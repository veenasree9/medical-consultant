⚡ Emergency Action Protocol (Check - Call - Care)

  1. Check Responsiveness: Tap the person firmly on both shoulders and shout loudly: "Are you OK?"
  2. Check Breathing: Look at the chest for 5-10 seconds for normal rising and falling. Gasping is NOT normal breathing.
  3. Call 112: If unresponsive and not breathing normally, call 112 immediately. Put phone on speaker.
  4. Hands-Only CPR: Place the heel of one hand in the center of the chest, interlock fingers, and push hard and fast (100–120 compressions/minute, 5 cm deep). Allow chest to fully recoil.
  5. Recovery Position: If breathing normally but unresponsive, turn the person onto their side to keep their airway open until help arrives.
