Manual Emergency First Aid & Rescue

Follow these standard healthcare first-responder instructions when a person cannot be identified or during an urgent crisis.

1. Immediate Priority: Call 112 Contact emergency dispatch immediately. Give your location and state of the person.
2. Airway & Breathing Check Gently tilt head back to open the airway. Look, listen, and feel for normal breathing. Do not give liquids to an unresponsive person.
3. Recovery Position (If Breathing) If breathing normally and no spinal injury is suspected, turn the person onto their side into the recovery position to prevent choking.
4. Severe Bleeding Control Apply firm direct pressure to open wounds using a clean cloth or bandage. Keep the person warm.
